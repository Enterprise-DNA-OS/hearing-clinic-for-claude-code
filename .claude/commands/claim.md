---
description: Raise a claim to a funder
---

Run `npm run clinic -- claim <client> --funder=hsp|msd|acc|hafs --item=".." --amount=.. [--date=service date] [--device=..]`. For HSP, the fitting claim waits until the client has accepted the aids at the follow-up.

Ambiguous names list the candidates and stop: ask which one. Never send anything; drafts stay in `drafts/`.
